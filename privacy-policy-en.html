Foody ("the App") values the importance of users' personal information and complies with the "Personal Information Protection Act" of the Republic of Korea and relevant laws of other countries. Through this privacy policy, we inform you about how the personal information you provide is used and what measures are taken to protect your personal information.

1. Personal Information Collected

The App does not collect any personally identifiable information. All data entered by users (food items, expiration dates, storage locations, etc.) is stored only on the user's device and is not transmitted to external servers.

2. Backup Functionality

The App provides a data export (backup) feature for user convenience. This backup data is stored in a location chosen by the user, and the responsibility for managing this data lies entirely with the user. The company does not store or manage backup files.

The App does not collect or use location information.
